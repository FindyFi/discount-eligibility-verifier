Prove you qualify for a discount, straight from your wallet

The verifier checks pensioner and student credentials presented from EUDI-compatible wallets over OpenID4VP. It trusts issuers through OpenID Federation and asks only for what the discount needs: no name and no date of birth.

Open the full-screen kiosk

  1. Get a credential

    A student status credential from Opintotodiste, or a pension credential from the Kela demo issuer. The wallet guide lists suitable wallets.

  2. Scan the code

    Scan the QR code with your wallet app. On a phone whose browser supports the Digital Credentials API, choose Use a wallet on this device instead.

  3. Approve in the wallet

    The wallet shows which details will be shared. Only the claims the eligibility rule needs are requested.

  4. See the result

    The card turns green or red. In the event log, verification-result carries the outcome.

What the verifier checks

      
A credential is accepted only when every check passes. When one fails, the result says which one and why.

    

    

      

        
Trusted issuer

        
The issuer must have a valid OpenID Federation trust chain to a configured trust anchor, such as Findynet's findy.trustregistry.eu. Individual did:web issuers can also be trusted directly.

      

      

        
Issuer signature

        
The SD-JWT credential must be signed with a key that the issuer publishes in its trusted metadata. Every disclosed claim must match its digest.

      

      

        
Holder binding

        
The wallet signs this request's nonce with the key the credential is bound to. A copied credential cannot be replayed.

      

      

        
Validity

        
The credential must be valid now. A rule can also require a date claim, such as a student card's expiry_date, still to be in the future.

      

      

        
Eligibility rule

        
The disclosed claims must meet the rule for the credential type, for example Voimassa: true for a pension or enrolment_status: enrolled for a student.

      

      

        
Signed result

        
The verifier signs the outcome for your backend. Grant the discount from that result, never from the browser alone.
